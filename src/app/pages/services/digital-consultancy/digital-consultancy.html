Digital Consultancy in Practice

Digital consultancy is independent advice for businesses facing decisions about technology, marketing, automation and AI. Sometimes you do not need a build, you need a clear view of what to do, in what order and what to avoid. We review where you are, explain the options in plain language and help you choose a path that fits your goals, your team and your budget, whether or not that involves us doing further work. A good consultancy engagement should leave you with more clarity and less noise. We avoid jargon, we explain our reasoning, and we are careful to separate what is essential from what is merely fashionable. You should finish the work knowing what to do next, why it matters and roughly what it will involve, so you can make confident decisions about where to spend your time and money.

Who this suits

Owners facing technology decisions

If you are weighing up platforms, suppliers or tools, an impartial view can prevent expensive mistakes.

Businesses curious about AI

If you want to use AI sensibly, we can help identify where it would genuinely help and where it would not.

Teams with an unclear plan

If digital activity feels scattered, we can help you set priorities and a practical roadmap.

What the work involves

  • Initial discovery

    We learn about your business, goals, current tools and challenges.

  • Review of your current setup

    We look at your website, systems and marketing to see what works and what is holding you back.

  • Clear recommendations

    We explain the options, the trade-offs and what we would do, in language you can share with your team.

  • A prioritised roadmap

    You receive a practical plan that puts actions in order, so you know what to do first.

  • AI opportunity assessment

    Where relevant, we assess where AI could save time or improve service, and where it is not worth the effort.

  • Follow-up support

    We can stay involved to help you review progress, answer questions and adjust the plan.

How we agree the work with you

Every Brandif project starts with a conversation, not a price list. We book a call, listen to what you are trying to achieve, and then agree the scope of work, the working schedule, the delivery date and the cost with you before anything starts. Because no two businesses need exactly the same thing, we do not sell fixed packages.

Consultancy works best when the question is clear, so the call is where we define it together. We discuss the decision you are facing, what you have already tried and what you need to come away with. We then agree the scope of the work, how long it will take, when you will receive the findings and the cost, and confirm it in writing.

Common questions

Will you tell us if we do not need your services?

Yes. Our advice is impartial, and if the right answer is to do less or to use another supplier, we will say so.

What will we receive at the end?

Typically a written summary of findings and a prioritised plan. We agree the exact format with you at the start.

Can you help us choose between platforms or suppliers?

Yes. We compare the options against your requirements and explain our recommendation.

How is consultancy priced?

The cost depends on the scope and time involved. We confirm it in writing after the initial call.

Do you only advise, or can you also do the work?

Both are possible. Some clients take our recommendations to their own team or another supplier, while others ask us to carry out the work. We keep the advice and any later work clearly separate, so you can decide each step freely.

Is the initial call free?

Yes. The initial call is a free conversation about your situation. It lets us understand the problem and lets you decide whether working with us makes sense before anything is agreed.
