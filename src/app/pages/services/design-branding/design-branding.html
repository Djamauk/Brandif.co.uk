Design and Branding in Practice

Good design makes a business easier to recognise, easier to understand and easier to trust. Branding is more than a logo. It is the combination of your name, visual identity, tone of voice and the way you present yourself in every place customers meet you. We help businesses define who they are and express it consistently, from a first logo to a full set of guidelines for your website, documents and social media. The best identities are simple, consistent and built to last. We pay attention to how a design will look in the places it will actually appear, such as a phone screen, a printed letter, a van or a social profile picture, and we test it there before we finish. That is how a brand ends up looking considered and dependable rather than merely decorative.

What the work involves

  • Brand discovery

    We talk through your business, audience, values and competitors so the design is based on strategy, not guesswork.

  • Logo and visual identity

    We design a logo, colour palette and typography that suit your business and work across print and digital.

  • Brand guidelines

    We document how to use your identity so anyone creating materials for you gets it right.

  • Key templates

    We create templates for items such as social posts, documents or presentations, as agreed.

  • Interface and layout design

    Where your project includes a website or app, we design the screens and layouts so they reflect the brand.

  • Final files and handover

    You receive the final files in the formats you need, along with a clear explanation of how to use them.

How we agree the work with you

Every Brandif project starts with a conversation, not a price list. We book a call, listen to what you are trying to achieve, and then agree the scope of work, the working schedule, the delivery date and the cost with you before anything starts. Because no two businesses need exactly the same thing, we do not sell fixed packages.

For design and branding, the call covers your business, who you want to reach, what you like and dislike, where the identity will be used and when you need it. We then agree the scope, the stages and review points, the delivery date and the cost, and confirm everything in writing.

Common questions

How many logo concepts do we get?

We agree the number of concepts and rounds of revisions with you at the start, so expectations are clear.

Do we own the final designs?

We agree ownership and usage terms in writing as part of the project, so there is no confusion.

Can you design for print as well as digital?

Yes. We prepare identities that work in both, and agree which materials are included.

Can you redesign our existing logo?

Yes. We can refresh an existing identity or start again, depending on what suits your business.

How long does a branding project take?

The schedule depends on the scope, the number of review rounds and how quickly feedback comes back. We agree the stages and the delivery date with you before the work begins, and we keep you updated as it progresses.

What do you need from us to get started?

A conversation about your business is the main thing. It also helps to share any existing logo or materials, examples of brands you admire, and a note of where the identity will be used.
